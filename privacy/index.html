Privacy Policy

Greenrise Energy Solutions India Private Limited (CIN U35106TZ2024PTC032814), Erode, Tamil Nadu, runs this website. This page explains what we collect when you contact us and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you enter in our contact or campaign forms: name, phone number, city or town, email, monthly electricity bill, project type and your message.
  • A random visitor ID stored in your browser, so we can tell repeat visits apart.
  • A human-verification check from Cloudflare Turnstile when you submit a form.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and follow up about solar, storage or PPA services. We do not sell your details.

Who sees it

Form submissions are stored in our internal customer-management system and are visible only to our team and the service providers that host it. We may share details with an installation or financing partner only when needed to deliver the service you asked for.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, by writing to office@greenriseenergy.com or calling +91 75984 83355.

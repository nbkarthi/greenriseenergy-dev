How We Deliver

Engineering & Delivery Standards

We don't have published case studies yet — here's how every project, large or small, is actually run.

Tier-1, ALMM-Listed Equipment

We spec panels and inverters from the official ALMM list — the same approved-manufacturer register that governs subsidy-linked and government-tendered installs.

Site-Specific Engineering

Shading, structural load and orientation are modelled per site before a quote goes out — not a generic system resized to fit your roof.

Single Point of Accountability

Permits, DISCOM liaison, procurement and commissioning all run through us — one team to call if anything needs chasing.

Flexible Financing

CapEx ownership, OpEx/PPA structures, or investor-funded builds — we scope the commercial model alongside the technical one.

O&M After Commissioning

Remote performance monitoring and scheduled maintenance, so an installed system stays accountable to its own output projections.

Compliance Handled

Net-metering applications, subsidy forms and CSR documentation are prepared and filed as part of the project — not left for you to figure out.

Our Process

How We Work

A streamlined 5-step approach from initial consultation to long-term performance monitoring.

1

Consultation & Site Assessment

We analyze your energy consumption, roof condition, shading analysis, and solar potential to determine the optimal system size and configuration for your property.

2

Custom Design & Proposal

Our engineers create a detailed system design using advanced PV modeling software. You receive a comprehensive proposal with performance estimates, ROI analysis, and financing options.

3

Engineering & Procurement

We handle all engineering drawings, permitting, and procure Tier-1 solar panels, inverters, and mounting systems from trusted manufacturers with full warranties.

4

Professional Installation

Our certified installation teams execute the project with precision, adhering to safety standards and best practices. Typical residential installations complete in 1-3 days.

5

Commissioning & Monitoring

After grid connection and system testing, we provide training and set up real-time monitoring. You can track energy production anytime via our mobile app.
